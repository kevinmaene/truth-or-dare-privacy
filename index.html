Privacy Policy

For the iOS app Truth or Dare · Last updated 19 August 2026

Truth or Dare has no accounts, no sign-in, and no server of its own. The developer does not collect, receive, or store any personal information about you. The game itself works entirely offline. The one exception is advertising: the app shows ads from Google AdMob, and Google may collect device and advertising data in order to serve them. That is described in full below.

Who this policy is from

This app is made by an independent developer trading as skepticalotterdev ("we", "us"). You can reach us at any time at skepticalotterdev@gmail.com.

What the app stores on your device

The app saves a small number of settings locally on your device, using Apple's standard UserDefaults storage, so the game remembers how you like to play:

  • whether 18+ (explicit) content is enabled
  • your chosen language
  • whether sound effects are on
  • whether the Elite difficulty has been unlocked

Player names and scores exist only while a game is running and are discarded when the game ends. None of this information is transmitted anywhere. It never leaves your device, we cannot see it, and deleting the app removes all of it.

Advertising

The app is free and is paid for by advertising. It uses Google AdMob to show two kinds of ads: an occasional full-screen ad during a game, and an optional ad you can choose to watch to unlock the Elite difficulty for that session. There are no banner ads.

To serve, measure, and prevent fraud in those ads, Google may collect and process data including your device's advertising identifier (IDFA), IP address, device type and operating system version, and information about ad interactions. We never see this data ourselves, and we receive no personal information about you from Google — only aggregate, anonymous reporting on how many ads were shown.

How Google uses data from apps that use its services is described here: google.com/policies/technologies/partner-sites.

Your consent choices

Two separate permissions govern the data above, and you control both.

  • Tracking (Apple's App Tracking Transparency). Before the system asks, the app explains why it is asking. If you decline, the advertising identifier is not used to track you and you will see less relevant ads. The game itself is completely unaffected — no feature, prompt, or difficulty is withheld based on your answer.
  • Ad consent (EEA and UK). If you are in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, a Google-provided consent form appears before any ads are served, letting you choose whether your data may be used for personalised advertising.

Sharing with third parties

Google AdMob is the only third party that receives any data from this app. We do not use analytics services, crash reporting, social network SDKs, or any other tracking tools, and we do not sell, rent, or share your data with anyone else.

Where user data is shared with a third party, that party is required to provide the same or equal protection of user data as is stated in this privacy policy and as required by the Apple App Store Review Guidelines.

Children

This app is not directed at children. It contains adult-oriented content — including sexual and alcohol-related prompts — which is kept switched off by default and can only be enabled after confirming that you are 18 or older. Please respect the age rating shown on the App Store listing.

Keeping, deleting, and revoking

We hold no data about you, so there is nothing for us to retain or delete on your behalf, and no account to close. You remain in full control:

  • Delete everything the app stored: delete the app from your device. All saved settings are removed with it.
  • Change or withdraw your ad consent (EEA/UK): open Settings inside the app and tap Ad privacy choices.
  • Change your tracking permission: open the iOS Settings app → Privacy & Security → Tracking, and turn the permission for Truth or Dare on or off at any time.
  • Reset your advertising identifier: the same iOS Privacy settings let you reset or limit the identifier Google uses.
  • Ask a question or make a request: email skepticalotterdev@gmail.com and we will respond. For data held by Google rather than us, Google's own privacy tools and policy apply.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this address with a new "last updated" date above. Material changes will also be reflected in the App Store listing.
